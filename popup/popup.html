    Enable external lookups?

    Domain ownership, known-vulnerability, and server-location checks send the site's domain / IP to third-party services (RDAP registries, NVD, crt.sh, ipwho.is) to get data that isn't available on the page itself. Header, cookie, TLS-basic, mixed-content, and library checks never leave your machine either way.

    You can change this later in the extension's settings.
